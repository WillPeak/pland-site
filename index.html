Not a calendar, not a budget app, not a chatbot. A household that runs itself, a bit more each week.

Saturday is the annoying one

You got through the week. The day is empty. Someone still has to invent it before breakfast. The night before was just getting to the end of the day, so nothing got planned. Now it's a museum, the park, or staying in, argued over while someone asks what's for breakfast.

It isn't only Saturday. School runs, gaps between things, the odd free hour. The week is already full. Pland's job is to use the gaps, not to fill every one of them, and to hold the result up against three dials.

Physical

Sleep, movement, and food. Plans are meant to follow recovery, not a streak.

Mental

One of the three dials, not a sticker added later. How you're actually doing sits on the same scoreboard.

Financial

Affordability comes from how money actually goes. No budget to type in and then abandon. Retirement odds are the longer view on this dial, and they're still where it's heading.

The sort of week

A session when recovery has room. Time with people, not only tasks. Dinner out only when the week can carry it. The dials are the scoreboard, not a second job.

Approval first

At first you approve. A plan worth keeping has a place, a time that's actually free, and a dial it's for. You keep it, or you throw it out. The direction after that: each kind of task goes hands-free once your history shows you'd keep it, and one bad call sends it back to drafting.

Where it's heading

The direction, not a list of what's already switched on.

  • Bookings through a partner
  • Occasions, with a secret gift on a single-use card
  • Talk
  • Texting Pland from iMessage
  • Capturing a flyer or a photo
  • Maths for the kids, and a wallet
  • A plan for the whole household that doesn't leave under-13s on their own
  • Plans that follow sleep and recovery
  • Retirement odds on the financial dial

Enough to plan

The calendar, how bodies are doing, and how money goes. Enough to shape a week. Not a dashboard you're supposed to babysit.
