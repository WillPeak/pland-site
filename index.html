Start with the money

No budget to keep. One number that decides what you can afford.

The financial dial is your chance of keeping today's way of life through retirement. Pland simulates 20,000 possible markets and counts how often your money lasts to age 95. Everything Pland suggests that costs money is checked against that number, not against a budget you have to maintain.

  • See what the chance rests on. Social Security, spending through the years and one-off costs are estimated, shown and changeable. Move retirement age, spending or saving and the chance and the chart update as you drag.
  • Spending and saving trade off. Raise one and the other falls by the same amount, so the levers show a real choice.
  • The planned year. Last year measured by kind, matched to the year before. Then this year's amount: the most the household can spend on what it chooses and keep the chance at 90% or better.
  • The household ledger. Every number traces back to the payment it came from.
  • Steady totals. Card charges that are still pending are kept out of classification, so a month's spending does not jump and drag the chance with it.
  • Pland Bank for kids. A child asks to spend, a parent approves by text, and Pland pays.

Retirement odds use the money you enter yourself.

How money meets the other two dials

A dinner out or an outing is suggested only when this month's share of the year's eating out and outings amount still has room. A trip is proposed only within what is left of the year's trips amount, after what is already booked. If the room is unknown, nothing is suggested. The mental and physical dials pick what is worth doing; the financial dial decides whether it fits.

The three dials

One scoreboard, not three apps

Fitness apps, budget apps and calendars each optimize their own number. A household has to live with all three at once. Pland weighs each plan against all three and shows what a choice does to each dial.

Financial

The simulated chance of keeping today's way of life. It sets what the household can afford.

Physical

From Apple Health VO2 max and the body scan. Workouts and meals are planned to move it.

Mental

From a short check-in built on standard questionnaires: GAD-7, PHQ-9 and WHO-5.

What works today

Shipped in the beta

Everything in this section is in the app now. Pland is in beta with a small number of households.

Move

  • Sized by today's recovery. WHOOP recovery sets how hard today's session is. Without a score Pland uses last night's sleep, then your recent workouts, then a moderate default.
  • Built for the time you have. Give a start and an end, and Pland builds the session, resting a muscle you trained today or yesterday.
  • Run in the app. Sets climb to your working weight. Cardio intervals are set by your VO2 max. A timer runs every part, with rest and what is next on screen.
  • The body scan. Measured on the phone, it feeds the physical dial. Only points and measurements are kept; no image leaves the phone.

Eat

  • Meals you can talk to. Say "tacos on Tuesday" or "the kids hate mushrooms" and the week's meals follow.
  • Photographed recipes, scaled per ingredient. Snap a recipe and each ingredient scales on its own: protein to the people eating, rice and pasta to what is left, the method word for word.
  • Batch cooking, only after you confirm. Cook enough for another dinner only when you say so. The next dinner then uses that pot.
  • One shopping list that follows the approved week. Change an approved week and the list is rebuilt from the plan as it stands.

Feel

  • A short check-in. The mental dial comes from it, so the score is something you answered, not something guessed.
  • Meditations written from your latest check-in.

Plan

  • A week for the whole household that never leaves an under-13 alone.
  • A morning iMessage digest. Reply to undo anything in it.
  • Text Pland from iMessage, or snap a flyer. Pland lists what it found, and you pick what goes on the calendar.
  • Vacation proposals. You approve one before anything is booked.

How it decides

Proposals, gates and evals

1

It proposes. You approve.

Nothing goes on the calendar, into the cart or onto a card without a tap from you. Proposals show what they do to the dials.

2

Exact confirms

Anything that spends money or commits time asks for an explicit yes on the specific thing: this vacation, this batch cook. A vague "sounds good" does not count.

3

Evals before release

Changes to how Pland decides run against evals built from past cases. A change that gets a known case wrong is caught before release.

4

Honest about what it used

When a signal is missing, the plan says what it used instead, like sleep in place of recovery, rather than pretending to know.

Where it is heading

Over time, more runs itself as it earns your trust.

Today you approve every plan. The direction is autonomy one job at a time: when your history shows you keep a kind of decision, Pland can offer to handle that job without asking. Each job earns it separately, and you can take it back.

The goal is a household where the week, the meals, the workouts and the money already fit together, and nobody has to invent Saturday.
