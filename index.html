Saturday is the annoying one

You got through the week. The day is empty. Someone still has to invent it before breakfast. The night before was just getting to the end of the day, so nothing got planned. Now it's a museum, the park, or staying in, argued over while someone asks what's for breakfast.

It isn't only Saturday. School runs, gaps between things, the odd free hour. The week is already full. Pland's job is to use the gaps, not to fill every one of them, and to hold the result up against three dials.

Physical

Movement and food. A streak is not the point. Workouts follow sleep and recovery from a connected wearable.

Mental

One of the three dials, not a sticker added later. How you're actually doing sits on the same scoreboard.

Financial

No budget to type in and then abandon. Retirement odds are your chance of keeping today's way of life, once you enter your money yourself.

The sort of week

Time with people, not only tasks. The dials are the scoreboard, not a second job.
