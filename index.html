What ships in beta

Focused on what families actually use

We’re not pretending to automate your entire life on day one. Here’s what’s real in the household beta.

Family calendar

Shared household calendar with Apple Calendar import, multi-person views, and instant local adds.

Life Planner

Auto-suggestions in open time — accept or dismiss. Built to avoid stacking on top of busy blocks.
