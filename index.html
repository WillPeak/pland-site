The problem we automate

Saturday morning, exhausted. No plan.

The classic failure mode: you and your partner worked hard all week. Young kids. You wake up Saturday with nothing on the calendar and no energy to invent a day — so you improvise. It’s fine-ish. Suboptimal. Draining.

Without Pland · Saturday 8:12am

Blank calendar. Decision fatigue. Ad‑hoc day.

Daycare week is over. You’re both wiped. Nobody planned Friday night because you were surviving the week. Now you’re negotiating a museum vs playground vs “just stay home” while the kids ask what’s for breakfast.

With Pland: the day was already on the calendar before you woke up — plans land ahead of the morning so you can shape them earlier if you want (you can still dismiss). Something that fits energy, the kids, and what the household can actually afford. No planning meeting. You weren’t the family project manager at 8am.

What health means here

Outcomes — not busywork.

Activities are how Pland tries. The dials are the scoreboard.

Mental

How you actually feel — from ongoing check-ins. Not “we had dinner, so you’re fine.”

Physical

Body outcomes — weight, fitness, composition from Health. Not “workouts logged.”

Financial

Quality of life you can keep — including a path to retire with it. Outings only when objective finances say they’re affordable. No typed dinner budget.

What shows up

What Pland puts into real life

Each loop fires from sensors, puts something concrete in your week, and is judged by whether a dial moved.

Household plans

When you’re free and it’s affordable, Pland fills open slots ahead of the day — whole-family plans, or clear splits where one parent matches the kids’ activity while the other works out or recharges. You shape them earlier if you want; you can still dismiss.

→ so a Saturday plan is on the calendar before you wake up

Workouts

When recovery says go, a timed in-app HIIT session lands in open time — voice coach talks you through every interval.

→ session in an open slot when recovery says go — judged by body outcomes, not streaks

Meals

A household meal plan for the week you’re living — portions, prep, and swaps — not random recipes.

→ week of food aimed at physical goals and the week you actually have

Trips & growth

Coming next: options that fit the calendar and what you can actually afford.

→ Next: options that fit time and affordability — you approve before anything is booked

Not yet

  • Want budgeting method or typed spending caps (use YNAB/Monarch)
  • Need Android/web or silent auto-booking today
