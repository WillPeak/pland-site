The problem we automate

Saturday morning, exhausted. No plan.

The classic failure mode: you and your partner worked hard all week. Young kids. You wake up Saturday with nothing on the calendar and no energy to invent a day — so you improvise. It’s fine-ish. Suboptimal. Draining.

Without Pland · Saturday 8:12am

Blank calendar. Decision fatigue. Ad‑hoc day.

Daycare week is over. You’re both wiped. Nobody planned Friday night because you were surviving the week. Now you’re negotiating a museum vs playground vs “just stay home” while the kids ask what’s for breakfast.

With Pland: the day was already on the calendar before you woke up — planned before you ask. You just approve or swap. Something that fits energy, the kids, and what the household can actually afford. No planning meeting. You weren’t the family project manager at 8am.

What we connect — and why

Calendar, Apple Health, and a money snapshot are inputs so Pland can act. They’re not the product. We don’t sell your data. You can dismiss any suggestion.

Calendar

So plans land where your household already looks — and open time is real.

Apple Health

Sleep and activity from your iPhone. Body-scan photos never leave your phone — only the measurements are saved.

Money snapshot

A money snapshot — savings, spending, income — so Pland only plans outings and trips when they’re affordable. No budget to set. Bank linking comes later.
